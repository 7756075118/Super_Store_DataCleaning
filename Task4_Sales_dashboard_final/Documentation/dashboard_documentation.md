## Task 4 - Data Visualization & Storytelling

---

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Title

**Sales Performance Analysis & Data Storytelling**

### Task

**Task 4 - Data Visualization & Storytelling**

### Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze sales and profitability data and create meaningful visualizations that communicate important business trends, patterns, insights, and recommendations.

An interactive Power BI report was developed using the cleaned Superstore dataset. The report presents key performance indicators, regional and category-level sales analysis, profitability analysis, and business recommendations.

## 4. Business Objective

The main objective of the dashboard is to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the overall sales and profit performance?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales?
3. Which product categories contribute the most sales?
4. Which regions generate the highest profit?
5. Which categories contribute the most profit?
6. Which areas have opportunities for improvement?
7. What business actions can be recommended based on the analysis?

# 6. Page 1 - Sales Performance Dashboard

## Purpose

This page provides an interactive overview of the company's overall sales and profitability performance.

## Key Performance Indicators

The following KPIs are displayed:

### Total Sales

$1.83M

Represents the total sales generated by the business.

### Units Sold

31K

Represents the total quantity of products sold.

### Total Profit

$442K

Represents the total profit generated by the business.

### Profit Margin

24.20%

Represents the percentage of sales retained as profit.

The Profit Margin was calculated using:
