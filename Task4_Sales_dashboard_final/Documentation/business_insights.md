## 3. Key Business Insights

### Sales Performance

- **West** is the highest-sales region.
- **Technology** is the highest-sales category.
- Technology generates approximately **$717K** in sales.

### Profit Performance

- **East** generates the highest regional profit.
- **Technology** generates the highest category profit.
- **Furniture** has comparatively lower profit performance.

### Discount Analysis

- Discount levels should be analyzed along with sales and profit.
- Excessive discounting may reduce profitability.

---

## 4. Key Findings

1. The business generated approximately **$1.83M in sales** and
   **$442K in profit**.
2. **West** leads in sales, while **East** leads in profit.
3. **Technology** is the strongest category for both sales and profit.
4. **Furniture** requires further investigation to improve profitability.
5. Discount strategies should be monitored to protect profit margins.

---

## 5. Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on Technology
Continue investing in high-performing Technology products.

### 2. Maintain West Region
Focus on customer retention and sales growth in the West region.

### 3. Analyze East Region
Study the product mix, pricing, and customer segments contributing to
East's strong profitability.

### 4. Improve Furniture Performance
Review Furniture pricing, discounts, costs, and product mix.

### 5. Monitor Discounts
Balance discounts with sales growth to avoid unnecessary profit loss.
