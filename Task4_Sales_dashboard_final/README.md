# Task 4 - Data Visualization & Storytelling

## Sales Performance Analysis

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data and creating
meaningful visualizations using Power BI.

The objective is to identify sales and profitability patterns, present
key business insights, and provide actionable recommendations through
a clear visual story.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing regions and categories.
- Analyze sales by sub-category.
- Understand regional and category-level profitability.
- Identify business improvement opportunities.
- Present insights using Power BI visualizations.

---

## Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report contains five pages:

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard
Provides an interactive overview of sales and profitability using KPI
cards, charts, and filters.

### 2. Overall Performance
Shows:
- Total Sales
- Units Sold
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Sales and Profit by Category

### 3. Sales Drivers
Analyzes:
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category

### 4. Profit Analysis
Analyzes:
- Profit by Region
- Profit by Category
- Average Discount by Category

### 5. Insights & Recommendations
Summarizes the major findings and provides business recommendations.

## Key Insights

- **West** is the highest-sales region.
- **East** generates the highest regional profit.
- **Technology** is the leading category for both sales and profit.
- **Furniture** has comparatively lower profit performance.
- Discount levels should be monitored to protect profitability.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Continue investing in the **Technology** category.
2. Maintain and expand sales in the **West** region.
3. Analyze the factors behind the **East region's** strong profitability.
4. Investigate pricing, discounts, and product mix in **Furniture**.
5. Monitor discount strategies to balance sales and profit.
